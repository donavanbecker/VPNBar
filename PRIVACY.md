# VPN Bar Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

VPN Bar doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data.

## What VPN Bar reads

VPN Bar reads the names and connection status of the VPNs configured on your Mac, so it can show them in the menu bar and Control Center. This information stays on your Mac.

## What VPN Bar stores

VPN Bar saves your preferences on your Mac, such as whether notifications are on, plus the time each VPN connected and which VPN you used most recently. This data never leaves your Mac and is deleted when you delete the app.

## Network connections

The Mac App Store version of VPN Bar makes no network connections of its own. The version downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub once a day for updates. That check sends no personal data.

VPN Bar doesn't provide a VPN service. When you connect a VPN, macOS and that VPN's own app handle the connection.

## Analytics and tracking

VPN Bar contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/donavanbecker/VPNBar/issues.
